# Initial product brief

Project: [Project name]<br>
Team: [Team members]<br>
Stakeholder: [Name, role, and organization]<br>
Faculty advisor: [Name]<br>
Updated: [Date]<br>
Status: Draft, not yet confirmed by the stakeholder

Replace the guidance with short paragraphs or bullets. Start with roughly one
page. Mark each claim as confirmed or assumed. On day one most of your brief
will be assumptions, and that's expected. Update the brief as you learn more.

## 1. Problem and users

Who has the problem, and why does it matter? Write the problem in one sentence
first.

## 2. Evidence

What do you know, and where did it come from? Separate what the stakeholder
said from what your advisor told you and from what you assume.

## 3. Desired outcome

What should improve, and how would you measure it?

## 4. Possible approaches

List two or three options. Include one that needs no new software. Don't choose
yet. You'll choose after you talk with your stakeholder.

## 5. Scope

What could a first version include and leave out? Note the constraints you know
about: data access, confidentiality, approved tools, and your stakeholder's
time.

## 6. Open questions

What still needs confirmation? These questions become the agenda for your first
stakeholder conversation.
