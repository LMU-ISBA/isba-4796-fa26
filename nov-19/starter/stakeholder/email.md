# Stakeholder email

This email asks your stakeholder for a first conversation. Send it when your
advisor says to, and not before. Some stakeholders hear from your advisor
first.

## Before you send

- Ask your advisor who your first contact is. The roster lists it.
- If your first contact is your advisor, skip this email. Hold your first
  conversation with your advisor, who brings in the stakeholder when it's time.
- If your advisor introduces you, reply to that introduction.
- Copy your faculty advisor.
- Send it from one teammate, with every teammate copied.
- Offer three specific times. Check them with your team first.
- Keep it short. Your stakeholder is doing you a favor in a busy month.

## Team version

Subject: LMU capstone team for [project], request for a short conversation

Dear [Name],

We're the LMU senior capstone team assigned to [project] for spring 2027. Our
faculty advisor is Prof [Name], copied here.

Our understanding so far is that [the problem in one sentence]. We'd like to
check that with you before we plan any work.

Could we meet for 20 to 30 minutes between Nov 30 and Dec 11? We're free
[option 1], [option 2], and [option 3]. We'd like to ask how the problem shows
up today, who it affects, and what a good result would look like.

We know this is a busy stretch. If it's easier, we could meet the first week of
the spring term, or send three questions by email.

Thank you for working with us.

[Full names]

## Internship or own-project version

Use this if your capstone is your internship. It goes to your manager.

Subject: Using my internship work for my LMU capstone

Dear [Name],

My LMU senior capstone runs in spring 2027, and I'd like to build it on my work
here. My faculty advisor is Prof [Name], copied here.

The problem I'd like to work on is [the problem in one sentence]. I'd like to
check that it's the right one, and that I'm allowed to work on it.

Could we talk for 20 minutes between Nov 30 and Dec 11? I'd like to ask what a
good result would look like, what information I can use, and what has to stay
inside the company.

If January is easier, that works too.

Thank you for considering this.

[Full name]

## If you don't hear back

Wait five business days, then reply to your own email with one line:

> I'm following up on the note below. Would a short call in early December or
> the first week of the spring term work for you?

Tell your advisor if a second note gets no reply.
