# Stakeholder confirmation

This note asks your stakeholder to confirm, in writing, that you're serving the
right person, solving the right problem, and measuring the right thing. It also
asks where the work can live.

## Before you send

- Send it after your first conversation, not before.
- Agree on the wording as a team first.
- Copy your faculty advisor.
- Save the reply in the `stakeholder` folder.
- If your stakeholder moved the conversation to January, send this in January
  and mark the brief as unconfirmed.

## The note

Subject: Confirming our understanding of [project]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for meeting with us on [date]. Before we plan the spring work, we'd
like to confirm four things with you.

1. Who we're serving. [Name and role] is the person this project should help
   most.
2. The problem. [The problem in one or two sentences.]
3. How we'll know it worked. [The measure, with today's number if you know it.]
4. Where the work lives. We keep project work in a private GitHub repository
   that only our team and our faculty advisor can open. At the end of the
   capstone, we hand the repository over to you. Is that acceptable, or does
   your organization need something different?

Could you reply with "confirmed," or tell us what to change? A correction helps
us as much as a yes.

Thank you again for your time.

[Full names]
