# Interview guide

Use this for your first stakeholder conversation. You have 20 to 30 minutes, so
plan for one real story and its follow-ups.

## Before

- Agree on who leads and who takes notes.
- Read section 6 of `PRD.md` and choose your three most important questions.
- Ask permission before you record or use an AI note-taker.

## The sequence

EXAMPLE → EXPLORE → REFLECT → NEXT

1. Example. Ask for a real story. "Tell me about the last time this happened."
2. Explore. Follow up with who, what, when, where, why, how, and how much.
3. Reflect. Play back what you heard, and let them correct you.
4. Next. Ask what you should look into next, and who else you should talk with.

A correction in step 3 sends you back to step 2. That means the interview is
working.

Don't ask a question that contains its own answer. "Is it slow because the files
are in three places?" tells them what to say. "What slows it down?" lets them
tell you.

## Cover these before you leave

- What would a good result look like, and how would you know?
- Who else does this affect?
- What information can we use, and what has to stay inside your organization?
- Is a private GitHub repository acceptable for our project work? We'd hand it
  over to you at the end.
- How much of your time can we plan on, and how should we reach you?

## After

- Copy `stakeholder/interview-notes-template.md` and name the copy with the
  date. Fill it in the same day.
- Update `PRD.md`. Mark a claim as confirmed only when the stakeholder said it.
- Send a thank-you note within a day.
- Send the note in `stakeholder/confirmation.md` once your team agrees on the
  problem statement.
